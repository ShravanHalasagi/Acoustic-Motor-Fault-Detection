
# Hardware Components

## 1. ESP32-S3 Development Board
- Acts as the main processing unit.
- Receives digital audio data.
- Performs embedded signal processing and AI inference.

## 2. INMP441 Digital Microphone
- Captures motor operating sounds.
- Provides digital audio output through the I2S interface.
- Supplies audio data for anomaly analysis.

## 3. Motor
- Acts as the machine under monitoring.
- Produces acoustic signals during operation.

## 4. Connecting Wires
- Establish electrical connections between the microphone and ESP32-S3.

## 5. Power Supply
- Provides the required power to the embedded system.

## Hardware Objective
To build a compact acoustic monitoring prototype using affordable embedded hardware.
