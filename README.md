Acoustic Motor Fault Detection Using Edge AI

1. Project Overview

Acoustic Motor Fault Detection is an embedded AI-based condition monitoring system designed to identify abnormal operating conditions in industrial motors by analysing their sound patterns.

The system uses an INMP441 digital microphone to capture motor sound signals and an ESP32-S3 microcontroller to process the audio data and perform lightweight anomaly detection at the edge.

The primary objective is to support early identification of potential motor abnormalities and improve predictive maintenance.

2. Problem Statement

Industrial motors can develop mechanical faults due to continuous operation, component wear, vibration and other operating conditions.

Traditional maintenance methods may fail to identify abnormalities at an early stage, resulting in unexpected downtime and increased maintenance costs.

This project explores acoustic monitoring as a cost-effective approach to identifying unusual motor operating patterns.

3. Proposed Solution

The proposed system combines digital audio sensing with embedded processing to monitor motor operating sounds.

- Captures real-time audio using the INMP441 microphone.
- Processes audio signals using the ESP32-S3.
- Analyses acoustic patterns to identify potential anomalies.
- Performs edge-based processing to reduce dependence on cloud infrastructure.
- Provides a foundation for continuous motor condition monitoring.

5. Working Principle

1. Audio Acquisition: The INMP441 microphone captures sound generated by the operating motor.
2. Signal Processing: The ESP32-S3 receives and processes the digital audio signals.
3. Feature Extraction: Relevant acoustic characteristics are extracted from the captured signals.
4. Anomaly Detection: The system analyses the extracted features to identify deviations from normal operating patterns.
5. Output: The detected condition can be communicated through an appropriate indication or monitoring interface.
