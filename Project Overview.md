
# Project Overview

## Project Title
Acoustic Motor Fault Detection Using Edge AI

## Introduction
This project focuses on detecting abnormal motor operating conditions through acoustic signal monitoring and embedded artificial intelligence.

## Problem
Traditional motor maintenance often depends on periodic inspection or physical measurements. Developing faults may remain unnoticed until they affect motor performance.

## Objective
To develop a compact, cost-effective system that monitors motor sounds and identifies unusual acoustic patterns.

## Proposed Approach
An INMP441 digital microphone captures motor audio signals. The ESP32-S3 processes the signals and performs AI-based anomaly detection locally.

## Expected Outcome
A working prototype capable of monitoring motor sounds and identifying acoustic anomalies.

## Real-World Impact
- Supports early fault identification.
- Helps reduce unexpected downtime.
- Provides additional information for maintenance planning.
- Supports cost-effective motor monitoring.
